🌐 Online Bibles

Download the public-domain translations OpenSanctuary hosts into your Bible library, or look up a passage or book. For any other Bible file or link (USFM, OSIS, Zefania), use Import → Bible from a Link or File.

Source: OpenSanctuary Bibles (public domain)

    📖 Look up a passage or a whole book

    Type a reference such as “John 3:16” or “Psalms 23”, or just a book name (“Jude”) to fetch every chapter. It is saved to your Bible library and added to the schedule.
