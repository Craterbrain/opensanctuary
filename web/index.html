📱 Connect & Pair Displays

Scan with your phone's camera to connect:

http://127.0.0.1:8080/remote

Ensure your phone or tablet is on the same Wi-Fi network. Point any regular phone camera or QR scanner at the code above to launch the remote.

📱 Open on This PC ↗ Stage Foldback ↗ Live FOH Display ↗

Scan to Authorize Sanctuary Display:

Scanning this code opens the camera scanner on your phone to pair your TV screen.

http://127.0.0.1:8080/pairing.html
Pairing Steps:
1. Launch Android TV or Roku app on the sanctuary display.
2. Point your phone camera at this QR code to launch pairing.html.
3. Point your phone camera at the TV screen to confirm pairing!
📷 Open Camera Tool ↗
On the TV:
1. Settings → Device Preferences → About → click "Build" 7 times to unlock Developer Options.
2. Developer Options → enable Network debugging.
3. Note the TV's IP address (Settings → Network & Internet).
4. The first time you connect, the TV will show a one-time "Allow debugging from this computer?" prompt — accept it with your remote.
This makes OpenSanctuary the TV's home app. The TV's own home screen is switched off, so the TV starts straight into OpenSanctuary and the Home button returns to it. It is not paired automatically: when it finishes, scan the code on the TV with your phone (the Pair TV App tab). Restore home screen below undoes the switch.

CCLI.com Login (optional)

Saved to your OS's own credential store (Keychain/Credential Manager/Secret Service), never as plaintext — used only to autofill the Upload Assistant window below. It still requires you to click Log In and Upload yourself.

What counts as "used"?
Only songs that actually went live during a service — adding a song to a schedule and never presenting it doesn't count.
Public Domain exclusion
A song is flagged PD when it has no CCLI number and its copyright field is empty or contains "public domain." This is a heuristic — double-check before relying on it.
Due date
Set in Settings → Integrations → CCLI Reporting Due Date.
